Why 8: treats the project covers as numbered museum plates (Plate I–IV). It's image-first, with the most visual appeal for anyone browsing, and uses the system's matted-plate treatment as the main idea. Why 8.5: laid out like an open book. On the left a fixed page carries your portrait, bio and navigation; on the right the projects scroll as entries with a cover and opening line. Who you are stays visible the whole time, which suits a portfolio built on psychology. It also carries straight into the case-study pages: the left page becomes the step list and the right holds the content. It's slightly behind 1a because the fixed left page needs a mobile fallback and narrows the content column.

Recommendation

Choose between 1a Folio and 1e Book Spread. Folio is the more distinctive first impression; Book Spread works best across the whole site, because its two-page structure carries into every case study. You could also use 1b's dark ground only for section dividers, which is how the system intends it.

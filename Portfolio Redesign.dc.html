About Me

Psychology, HCI, and a camera

I'm a UX enthusiast with a B.A. in Psychology and Human-Computer Interaction, passionate about blending psychological insights with research to design intuitive, engaging experiences. I love uncovering what drives user behavior and using that understanding to create designs that feel effortless and human-centered.

Outside of UX, I'm a dedicated cinephile who enjoys analyzing films almost as much as watching them. One of my favorite design analogies comes from Inception of Christopher Nolan:

"We create the world of the dream. We bring the subject into that dream, and we fill it with their subconscious."

Dom Cobb

To me, great UX is just like that — building a world shaped by the user's needs, then guiding them through it seamlessly.

In my free time, I enjoy the mental challenge and social energy of board games, the calming rhythm of early-morning water sports, and capturing candid human moments through photography during my travels. The lens helps me see the world — and people — in a more intentional, creative way.
